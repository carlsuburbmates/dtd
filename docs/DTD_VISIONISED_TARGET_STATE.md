# DTD Visionised Target State

**Purpose:** longer-term possibilities.
**Authority:** none of these items is an implementation commitment until moved into the targeted state through an explicit decision.

## Product and market evolution

- Demand-tiered suburb pricing after reliable traffic and conversion data exists.
- Active Melbourne-Wide reach marketing after at least half of the 539 eligible suburb pages are confirmed indexed and a real sponsor has at least 60 days of usage evidence.
- Annual Suburb and Melbourne-Wide plans, quarterly plans, team products, optional trust/verification products, or paid owner resources only after volume and support burden justify them.
- Broader Victorian or Australian geography only after Greater Melbourne data, operations and economics are stable.

## Automation evolution

- A trigger evaluator that watches approved growth thresholds and notifies the operator only when a decision is due.
- Licensed-feed acquisition and refresh loops that remain source-aware, suppression-first and observable from `/ops`.
- Better anomaly detection for provider failures, supply gaps, billing drift and content/indexation decay.
- Assisted recommendations that improve fit evidence without permitting payment to alter substantive suitability.

## Distribution and ecosystem evolution

- Deeper but opt-in First Leash handoff formats, such as user-controlled encrypted dossier export, only with a new privacy and integration decision.
- Co-branded education links for trainers without merging the two applications or transmitting behavioural data by query string.
- Mobile or partner distribution only when it reduces real user friction enough to justify solo-operator maintenance.

## Explicitly retired vision

- Appsmith is not the future operator layer. DTD already owns a bespoke `/ops` surface; future operator work extends or simplifies that surface instead of creating a parallel system.
- Google Places/Maps content, Search Grounding, generic SERP scraping and unapproved directories are not a shortcut to autonomous trainer inventory.
