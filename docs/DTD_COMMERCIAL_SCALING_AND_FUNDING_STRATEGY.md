## 3. Four connected growth and revenue workflows

### Engine 1 — Useful local discovery and suburb SEO

Use the existing `/melbourne/:suburb` route and canonical catalogue; this strategy does not introduce a parallel `/suburbs/` system. Publish/index only where the [SEO contract](specs/SEO_AND_INDEXATION.md) and CDR-013 permit: at least three eligible published trainers and 500 words of qualifying content, with correct canonical/noindex/sitemap state. The 539-suburb geography catalogue is not 539 supplied or indexable markets.

Structured data must match visible, evidenced facts. Do not invent ratings, testimonials, local supply or professional verification for search appearance. Organic traffic, ranking, conversion and acquisition cost must be measured; neither free traffic nor zero customer-acquisition cost is guaranteed.

### Engine 2 — Guided matching and explicit owner contact

Follow Decision Contract v2: validated current trainer capacity determines eligibility; AI reasons about fit within those candidates; deterministic presentation preserves the approved 0.05 comparable-fit band. Keep thin-supply, weak-evidence, no-match, urgent-support and AI-degradation paths explicit.

The established questionnaire contract governs fields; this strategy does not add budget, timing or new service formats. A result is not an enquiry or authority to broadcast personal details. The owner explicitly chooses and consents to the contact path; persistence, notification, retry/fallback and `/ops` evidence must agree with the visible outcome.

### Engine 3 — Free claim, capability confirmation and optional Pro

A legitimate trainer may claim and manage an eligible Core listing and receive direct enquiries at A$0. Verify through the current business-email OTP/session workflow, reconcile provenance, and confirm structured services/catchments. Public visitors do not see owner management controls; returning trainers recover a valid session before editing. SMS verification is not introduced or represented as accepted by this strategy.

After experiencing useful enquiries, the trainer may choose an optional upgrade. **Claiming alone does not start a paid subscription or Pro trial.** Under the pricing contract, an eligible first-time Pro subscription starts its provider-backed 30-day trial on Stripe subscription creation; live cohort admission requires the authorised anchor and qualifying registration/claim timestamp. Test-mode and pre-anchor records do not silently enter the live cohort. Persist dates/consumption, send the day-23 warning once, and verify cancellation, portal, failure, refund and notification states.

Do not sell “Verified Professional” status or present paid status as professional accreditation. Pro visibility must preserve paid-neutral fit; free/Core enquiry access must not become a new paywall. Existing approved products remain Core A$0, Pro A$19/month or A$149/year, Suburb Sponsorship A$39/month, and Melbourne-Wide A$199/month.

### Engine 4 — Honest sponsorship availability and inventory

Preserve two active businesses per suburb, four sponsored suburbs per business by default, and five Melbourne-Wide positions. Show actual persisted availability; no fabricated scarcity or promise of top position for every search/enquiry. Commercial presentation applies only within the locked comparable-fit band.

Verify reservation expiry, concurrency, duplicate protection, provider payment result, entitlement, cancellation/refund release and exceptions together. An available slot or a successful test checkout does not establish paid demand or a complete commercial release.
